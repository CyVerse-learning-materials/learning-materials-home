    AI Verde MCP Servers¶

    AI Verde Model Context Protocol (MCP) Servers provide a crucial bridge, allowing Generative AI Agents and large language models (LLMs) to securely and intelligently interact with the rich data and computational resources of CyVerse Infrastructure. This mechanism enables AI workloads—such as RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) applications, advanced chatbots, and data analysis pipelines—to access research datasets stored in the CyVerse Data Store and utilize other key services.
